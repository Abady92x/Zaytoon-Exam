📚 Chapter 4-1: Hazard Classification & Process Safety
Q1: The following is correct about hazardous area classification:
(a) Division 1 is more dangerous than Division 2
(b) Class III is more dangerous than Class I & II
(c) Group D is more dangerous than Group A
(d) Dusts and fibers are classified Class I
Division 1 represents hazardous conditions more likely during normal operation.
Q2: The following is correct about the use of industrial trucks in hazardous areas:
(a) LP and LPS have similar safety features
(b) DY has less safety features than D
(c) EX is safer than ES
(d) EE is the safest industrial truck
EE trucks have fully enclosed electric systems for maximum hazard protection.
Q3: The following industrial trucks can be used in Class I, Group B, Division 1:
(a) EX only
(b) DY, EE & EX
(c) DS, DY, ES
(d) None of the above
These three truck types have the required safety features for this hazard class.
Q4: The following unit has Class II hazardous location:
(a) Gasoline storage tank
(b) Aluminum powder storage area
(c) Textile plant
(d) Gas storage tanks
Class II involves combustible dust hazards - aluminum powder is a classic example.
Q5: NOT true about process knowledge principles:
(a) Process knowledge should remain useful
(b) Support efforts to properly manage change
(c) Protect knowledge from being accessed by workers
(d) Protect knowledge from inadvertent loss
Knowledge SHOULD be accessible to workers for safety and efficiency.
Q6: NOT true about process knowledge principles:
(a) Protect and update process knowledge
(b) Use process knowledge
(c) Maintain an undependable practice
(d) Catalog knowledge for easy retrieval
We must maintain DEPENDABLE practices, not undependable ones.
Q7: NOT true about HIRA application:
(a) HIRA can't be applied during operation phase
(b) HIRA is best applied earlier in lifecycle
(c) Earlier application makes it more cost effective
(d) Common activities in both HIRA and risk assessment
HIRA CAN be applied during operations for continuous improvement.
Q8: Quantitative HIRA technique:
(a) HAZOP
(b) What-If
(c) FTA
(d) FMEA
FTA (Fault Tree Analysis) uses mathematical operators to calculate failure probabilities.
✅ True or False Questions
1. Process knowledge management means creation and organizing information.
Correction: Management includes creating, organizing, maintaining, AND providing information.
2. Poor process knowledge negatively affects process safety competency.
Correct! Poor knowledge undermines the ability to operate safely.
3. D, G & LP trucks are not allowed in ANY hazardous area.
Correction: They're prohibited in SOME areas (Class I Division 1) but allowed in others.
4. All RBPSM activities should be based on process knowledge.
Correct! Dependable practice is built on solid process knowledge.
5. What-If analysis needs only one person to conduct.
Correction: This is a group-based technique requiring multiple participants.
6. Formal Safety Reviews are for small process changes.
Correction: Used for new processes and substantial changes, not small ones.

✅ LOPA True/False

Correct! Bow-tie diagrams identify scenarios for LOPA analysis.

Correction: Not all safeguards meet IPL requirements (independence, effectiveness, etc.).

Correct! Independence is critical to maintain layer effectiveness.
